How The Recommendation Algorithm Works
  • Full Listens & Replays (+3): High-replay tracks enter In Heavy Rotation and weight future mixes.
  • Liked Songs (+5): Primary anchor for My Supermix (60% favorites + 40% discovery).
  • Early Skips (-2): Skipping before 15s demotes the song and reduces artist frequency in Automix.
  • Automix Scoring: Prioritizes similar songs based on cosine similarity with your taste vector.
